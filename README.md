## 🧩 Core Modules That Redefine the Experience

### ⚙️ **Reactive Resource Governor**
Your CPU doesn't need to work at 100% for a menu screen. Our governor dynamically allocates processing power based on on-screen action density. Walking through a quiet forest? Resources flow elsewhere. Mid-boss-fight with particle effects everywhere? Every core channels energy into the encounter.

### 🧠 **Latency Smoothing Engine (LSE)**
Not all delays come from your network. System-level interrupt handling can introduce micro-stutters that break your rhythm. The LSE acts like a **traffic controller for your PC's data highways**, prioritizing combat-relevant system calls during intense sequences.

### 🎛️ **Thermal Equilibrium Profiler**
Heat is the silent assassin of performance. Our profiler monitors temperature curves and adjusts clock behavior strategically, preventing thermal throttling *before* it ruins your perfect dodge timing.

### 📊 **Input Precision Calibrator**
Every mouse and keyboard has a personality—dead zones, acceleration quirks, polling rate inconsistencies. This module maps your input device's fingerprint and compensates, making every keystroke feel like a direct extension of your thought.

### 🪞 **Memory Lane Optimization**
RAM efficiency is the unsung hero of smooth gameplay. We clean up memory fragmentation patterns during loading screens, ensuring that when the action starts, every byte is where it needs to be.

### 📈 **Session Performance Analytics**
Knowledge is power. After each session, you receive a digestible breakdown of system performance metrics, visualized in elegant charts. See where your machine struggled, what environments were heaviest, and how the optimizer adjusted—all in a beautiful, human-readable format.
